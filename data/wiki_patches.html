Patches

From Final Fantasy XIV Online Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Patches are content updates introduced by Square Enix. Since Endwalker, major patches (x.1, x.2, etc.) are released on a 4.5-month (19-week) interval. The time between the final major patch of an expansion (x.5) and the next expansion is approximately 9 months.

Final Fantasy XIV 1.0

  • Closed Alpha test. 11 March 2010
  • Open Beta test. 2 September 2010
  • PC-only release with "temporarily" suspended subscription fees. 30 September 2010
